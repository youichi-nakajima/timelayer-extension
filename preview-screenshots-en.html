💡 Manual 1280 × 800 px Capture via Chrome DevTools

In addition to the 1-click download button, you can capture pixel-perfect 1280 × 800 screenshots directly using Chrome DevTools:

  1. Press F12 to open Chrome DevTools.
  2. Press Ctrl + Shift + M to toggle the Device Toolbar.
  3. Set the viewport dimensions at the top to 1280 × 800 (100% zoom).
  4. Press Ctrl + Shift + P to open the Command Menu.
  5. Type "Capture screenshot" and press Enter to save the exact PNG.
